                                                                  6. AI Natives are not competing head-on with SaaS incumbents, they are winning
                                                                     by occupying the white space between them. As software value shifts from
                                                                     systems of record to systems of action, the center of gravity moves away from
                                                                     databases and dashboards toward agents that resolve problems end‑to‑end.
                                                                     Incumbent SaaS products were built around discrete functional silos while AI Natives
                                                                     are built around outcomes. AI Natives are winning with packaged solutions that go
                                                                     live in weeks, convert proofs of concept at high rates, and monetize on business
                                                                     impact rather than seat count or feature breadth.

                                                                     inflection point in 2022. While meaningful commercialization may still be 5-10
                                                                     years out, there are meaning advances happening in Applied AI as progress in

                                                                     intelligence and real world action, creating durable software-defined platforms
                                                                     across logistics, labor automation, and defense. The critical shift is that intelligence is
                                                                     no longer confined to perception or decision support, but embedded directly into
                                                                     machines that act, learn, and improve in continuously changing environments.
                                                                     Across verticals, winners are those with sustained real world deployments that
                                                                     generate proprietary data flywheels, enabling compounding performance

                                                  Archera

                                                                        We hosted Archera’s CEO and Co-Founder, Aran Khanna. Archera helps businesses
                                                                        manage and optimize cloud spend through AI-driven forecasting and automation,
                                                                        FinOps insights, and insured commitments. Through offerings such as Insured
                                                                        Commitments, Guaranteed Savings Plans (GSPs), and Guaranteed Reserved Instances
                                                                        (GRIs), Archera enables businesses to secure cloud discounts with flexible terms,
                                                                        reducing costs risks associated with traditional long-term cloud commitments.

                                                                        n   AI has caused a combination of supply constraints and increased uncertainty
                                                                            that has increased the complexity of purchasing cloud compute. This has made
                                                                            customers hesitant to commit to longer term contracts but also increased the
                                                                            leverage CSPs have. This has resulted in hyperscalers raising prices on certain
                                                                            high‑end GPUs (H200 & GB200) and limiting commitment availability, forcing
                                                                            customers into bidding on spot‑markets depending on supply‑demand conditions.
                                                                            Overall the massive increase in cloud computing spend has increased the market
                                                                            opportunity for Archera.

                                                  Automation Anywhere

                                                                        We hosted Automation Anywhere’s Co-Founder & CEO, Mihir Shukla. Automation
                                                                        Anywhere is a provider of AI-powered automation, providing an enterprise-grade
                                                                        platform that combines traditional Robotic Process Automation (RPA) with advanced
                                                                        generative AI and agentic capabilities. Automation Anywhere enables organizations to
                                                                        deploy intelligent digital workers and autonomous AI agents that can reason, make
                                                                        decisions, and execute complex business processes across fragmented legacy systems
                                                                        and modern cloud applications.

                                                                        n   SaaS disruption (the agent layer eats the UI layer). Mr. Shukla makes the claim that
                                                                            AI will effectively re-bundle SaaS. If agents can navigate, reconcile, and execute



                                                  15 April 2026                                                                                                   9

                                                                      across systems, the value migrates away from human-facing workflows toward
                                                                      systems-of-record + APIs - while an orchestration layer coordinates everything
                                                                      above it. In that world, enterprises don’t want hundreds of AI applications and

                                                                  n   AI as labor arbitrage + throughput unlock. Mr. Shukla noted the practical unit of

                                                                  n   Long approval cycles are a main bottleneck to AI adoption. Companies can stand
