                                                                     n     Government expenditure is set to rise by a historic pace of 12.8% to KRW821trn in
                                                                           2027 (Exhibit 2). Budget allocation for general government (including local tax
                                                                           allocation and general public administration) is set to increase sharply by
                                                                           KRW27.6trn and account for roughly 30% of the headline expenditure increase.
                                                                           Spending on healthcare, welfare, labor and education is also set to collectively
                                                                           increase KRW35.4trn and account for roughly 40% of the headline sequential
                                                                           increase. In comparison, contribution from investment-related spending hikes (R&D
                                                                           and industry support) would be relatively moderate at 15%. Overall, we estimate the
                                                                           fiscal impulse from stronger expenditures could amount to 1.0pp to headline GDP
                                                                           growth. Given the roughly +0.7pp already reflected in our GDP forecast, we see




                                                                                                                                                                                                                    455849510b1242a59217c821d3d1e367
                                                                           additional potential upside of roughly 0.3pp to our 2027 growth forecast coming
                                                                           from strong government spending.
                                                                     n     Government revenue is also projected to rise by a historic pace of 30.4% to
                                                                           KRW880.8trn. The increases in revenues from 2026 would be about twice as much
                                                                           as increases in expenditures. The sharp improvement is driven almost entirely by
                                                                           stronger tax revenues, which are to rise 49.8% to KRW584.4trn in 2027.

                                                                     n     The government also disclosed its plan for managing the Future Response Fund (FRF)
                                                                           in 2027 (Exhibit 3). Primary funding for the FRF will come from excess tax revenues
                                                                           of KRW162.3trn (defined as revenues exceeding past ten-year’s average growth),
                                                                           mostly from semiconductor corporate tax incomes. Out of the excess tax receipts,
                                                                           the central government budget will allocate KRW52.9trn to the spending account for
                                                                           four major policy projects carried out through the FSF, including KRW45.4trn
                                                                           earmarked for spending in 2027 and the rest retained as reserve funds within these
                                                                           accounts. The rest (KRW109.4trn) will be allocated to a general account (i.e., not tied
                                                                           to specific projects), of which KRW12.5trn will be used to reduce new KTB issuance.
                                                                           In total KRW104.4trn (KRW7.5trn in the 4 major policy area account and

                                                                           KRW96.9trn in the general account; amounting to 3.3% of government projected
                                                                           nominal GDP) would be reserved for future use, likely beyond 2027. That said, the
                                                                           government retains some flexibility to raise spending (by 30% under proposed fiscal
                                                                           rules) without approval of a supplementary budget by the National Assembly.
