                                                                         Key Takeaways

                                                                         n   Following several quarters of generally constructive reactions to expansionary
                                                                             events, Q1 2026 saw negative reactions across most AI stocks with AI Empowered
                                                                             names being the most volatile. AI Empowered names have been the most pressured
                                                                             over our analysis period likely on the back of debates surrounding AI
                                                                             disintermediation risks while AI Enablers have on average been positive given the
                                                                             direct implications from Hyperscaler capex growth. Our crowding measures (Exhibit
                                                                             2) also point to AI Empowered being relatively less crowded relative to AI Enablers.
                                                                         n   Idiosyncratic factors have been significant in explaining stock moves as evident in




                                                                                                                                                                            455849510b1242a59217c821d3d1e367
                                                                             January earnings-day moves for MSFT (-10%), AMZN (-6%), GOOGL (-1%) and META
                                                                             (+10%) despite all being “capex expansionary” events. MSFT posted another
                                                                             consecutive quarter of higher-than-expected capex without a commensurate
                                                                             increase in Azure growth rates, AMZN was subject to scrutiny in terms of how the
                                                                             capex increased could improve its rate of AWS revenue & backlog growth and META
                                                                             showed accelerating momentum in its core advertising business along with an
                                                                             increasing capex.
                                                                         n   Investor expectations and broader sentiment play an important role as seen in
                                                                             negative stock reactions on some capex expansionary events; for example, Q1 2025
                                                                             saw negative reactions across AI stocks when the ROI debate intensified while
                                                                             sentiment turned more positive in the following quarter.
                                                                         n   One-month normalized put-call skew for these names have seen a significant
                                                                             increase over the past week suggesting a relatively more bearish investor positioning
                                                                             but remaining below extreme fear levels seen during April 2025.

                                                                             companies that have increased AI capex have turned negative recently.
